Welcome to Empirical Research Methods for Computer Science, Autumn Semester (HS) 2026!

This course introduces core principles for designing, analyzing, and critically evaluating empirical studies in computer science. It focuses on how to formulate research questions, select appropriate study designs, collect and analyze evidence, and draw valid and interpretable conclusions.

#### Overview

The course is relevant to empirical questions across different areas of computer science. 
Example questions might include evaluating whether a new machine learning method improves prediction quality across datasets; benchmarking the performance or scalability of a systems technique; comparing the effectiveness of software testing or program analysis approaches; experimentally evaluating a security mechanism or measuring security behavior in practice; studying how people interact with an AI-enabled system; or conducting interviews and field studies to understand how computing technologies are adopted and used. 
The emphasis is not on any particular application area, but on the methodological principles needed to design credible empirical studies and draw defensible conclusions from evidence.
