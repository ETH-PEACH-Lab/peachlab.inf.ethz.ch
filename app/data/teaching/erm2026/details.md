The course is held in six 2-hour sessions and a poster session. Students are allowed to miss one session without penalty. The course concludes with a poster presentation, where students present their proposed empirical study.

#### Intended Audience

This course is intended for **PhD students and advanced Master's students in computer science** who are interested in empirical research. Students should already have a basic understanding of research in their area, for example through prior coursework, a research project, or thesis preparation.

The course is particularly relevant for students who are **planning to design and conduct empirical studies as part of their thesis or research**.
