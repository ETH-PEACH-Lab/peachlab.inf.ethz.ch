#### Grading

- Final Project (proposal + poster): 50%
- Exam: 40%
- Attendance: 10%

##### Final Project

Throughout the course, students will develop and iteratively refine a proposal for an empirical study. At the end of the course, they will present the proposal in a poster session. The proposal should clearly articulate the research question, study design, measurement and data collection strategy, analysis plan, and key validity considerations.

##### Exam

Towards the end of the semester, we will organize a digital exam covering the core concepts and reasoning skills introduced in the course. Students are allowed to bring one A4 cheat sheet, printed or handwritten on both sides.

##### Attendance

Each lecture will have a sign-in sheet. Attendance at each lecture counts for 2% of the final grade, up to a maximum of 10%. This means that students may miss one of the six lectures without penalty, but additional absences will reduce the attendance grade.

There is no required homework. Optional readings may be provided after each lecture for students who would like to explore the topics in more depth. Lecture notes and slides will be made available, but lecture recordings will not be provided.
